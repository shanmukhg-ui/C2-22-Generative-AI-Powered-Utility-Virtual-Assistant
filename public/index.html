Problem Statement & Target Users

Utility organizations (electricity, water, gas, waste, telecom) maintain vast amounts of operational, procedural, and customer data scattered across complex portals and CRM silos. Customers face high friction looking up simple connection procedures, resulting in call center backlogs.

• Citizens: Connection guidelines, tariff rules, ticket tracking.
• Field & Office Staff: Fast access to internal SOPs and daily outage summaries.
• Supervisors & Admins: Incident aggregation, audit logs, and knowledge accuracy.
Mentor Feedback Compliance:
  • Synthetic schemas validated against real-world utility CRM tables.
  • Clear fallback mechanism implemented when similarity score < 0.25.
  • End-to-end RAG retrieval & generation latency benchmarked under 3 seconds.

Key Deliverables & Objectives

This virtual assistant overcomes acute information fragmentation across municipal utility departments. It connects natural-language queries directly to verified procedural circulars via grounded RAG and executes real-time database queries for ticket tracking.

Strict Grounding All procedural answers reference official document IDs and cited circular text to eliminate LLM hallucinations.
Enforced RBAC Role-Based Access Control isolates customer records while empowering operational staff with daily telemetry summaries.
Mentor Sign-off Note: Latency target under 3 seconds verified. Synthetic CRM schemas match real-world utility data formats. Fallback mechanism triggers gracefully on low-confidence queries.
